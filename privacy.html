Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Purpose

Binance Historical Archive is a personal utility for storing copies of publicly available Binance historical market data in the operator's Google Drive.

Google account access

The utility requests Google Drive access through Google OAuth. It uses this authorization to upload, read back, and verify archive files in the Google Drive account authorized by the operator. The currently configured OAuth permission may allow broader Drive access than the archive workflow itself requires.

Information handling

The utility processes publicly available market-data archives and technical information needed to operate and verify transfers, such as file names, checksums, transfer status, and operational logs. Google OAuth credentials and tokens are used for authorization and must not be published in this repository.

Sharing

The utility is intended for the operator's own use, not for selling personal information or providing a public user-data service. Google Drive data is processed by Google under Google's applicable terms and policies.

Control and revocation

The Google account holder can revoke the application's access through Google Account connections. The operator can also delete stored archives from their Google Drive.
