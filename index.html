Binance Historical Archive

Binance Historical Archive is a personal, non-commercial archiving utility used by its operator to collect publicly available Binance historical market-data files and store backup copies in the operator's Google Drive.

Google OAuth is used solely to authorize the utility to access the Google Drive account selected by the operator for archive storage. The application is not affiliated with Binance or Google.

This site is informational; it does not offer public account registration or trading services.
